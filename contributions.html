Interpretation boundary

Reach is context.
Acceptance is evidence.

Repository stars and forks describe the upstream project, not Rudy’s share of its creation. A contributor rank is GitHub’s current API ordering, not a qualitative review. Merged PRs and accepted line changes describe accepted public work, but volume alone does not establish importance.

Use the individual columns when you need a specific signal. Use the balanced view only as a convenient, reproducible way to explore several signals together.
