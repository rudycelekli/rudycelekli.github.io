swe-sweep · Accounting for grader crashes in SWE-Sweep

Rudy Celekli’s engineering portfolio · AI-assisted summary

Problem

A grader crash writes a short reward record with a failure marker. The score reader expected fields from a completed trial, so a crashed trial could vanish from evaluation failure counts.

Engineering response

The scorer checks the explicit grader_failed marker before requiring completed-trial fields. It reports the crash and keeps the trial out of the headline score.

Evidence and scope

The merged test plan covers scoring and grader-failure records, including the distinction between a crash and a file that is merely incomplete.
