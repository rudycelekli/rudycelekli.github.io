RuVector · Rejecting malformed vector segments in RuVector WASM

Rudy Celekli’s engineering portfolio · AI-assisted summary

Problem

Opening an invalid RVF buffer could skip a malformed vector segment or fall back to a one-dimensional store, leaving callers with a store that misrepresented the data.

Engineering response

The WASM open path fails if a vector segment is truncated, lacks a usable dimension or disagrees with a preceding segment.

Evidence and scope

The merge record notes an initial local linker limitation and reports a later 17-test pass on the merged tree. It also records a supply-chain check failure shared with its comparison run on main.
