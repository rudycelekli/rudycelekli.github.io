Rudy Celekli’s engineering portfolio · AI-assisted summary

Problem

A Policy Lab export accepted a submitted repair report without confirming its relationship to the underlying search. A substituted bundle or altered minimality claim could therefore appear as valid evidence.

Engineering response

Rerun the bounded local search at export, compare canonical digests across the full report, and export the recomputed result when they match. Reject discrepancies before creating output.

Evidence and scope

The pull request reports four baseline failures and 82 passing Policy Lab tests after the change, including the CLI export route. The checks use synthetic offline evidence.
