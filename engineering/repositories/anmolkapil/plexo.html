plexo · Keeping spaces in filenames downloaded by Plexo

Rudy Celekli’s engineering portfolio · AI-assisted summary

Problem

Some servers send Content-Disposition filenames with spaces and without quotes. Plexo stopped at the first space, turning quarterly report.csv into quarterly.

Engineering response

The parser consumes the unquoted value through the next parameter separator and trims the surrounding spaces and tabs. Quoted and extended filenames continue to use their existing paths.

Evidence and scope

An Electron Playwright regression reproduced the truncated name. The merged record reports 32 passing download and redirect checks with the correction.
