AiSOC · Reading all available Entra audit pages in AiSOC

Rudy Celekli’s engineering portfolio · AI-assisted summary

Problem

The Entra connector consumed only the first response page from Microsoft Graph. More than 200 matching audit or risky-sign-in entries in a poll could leave older entries unread before the next polling window.

Engineering response

The connector follows the supplied @odata.nextLink for both feeds. It retains completed pages when a later request fails and warns at the 25-page limit.

Evidence and scope

A focused fixture retrieved 680 entries across both feeds and reproduced first-page loss when the source change was reverted. The merge record also notes existing test-environment failures and the absence of a live tenant run.
