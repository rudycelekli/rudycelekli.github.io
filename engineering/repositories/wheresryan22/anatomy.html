anatomy · Reject star polygons at the convex prism boundary

Rudy Celekli’s engineering portfolio · AI-assisted summary

Problem

Anatomy's prism builder checked whether vertex turns had consistent signs. A regular star can satisfy that condition while winding twice, violating the assumptions of convex-part rendering and collision logic.

Engineering response

Require one complete turn around the ring and ignore repeated vertices in the turn calculation. Convex rings, collinear points and ordinary rounded rectangles retain their valid paths.

Evidence and scope

The PR reports two failing star-polygon cases on the baseline and all 30 checks passing across the relevant geometry suites. Generated examples were rebuilt with unchanged output.
