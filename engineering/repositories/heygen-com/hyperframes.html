hyperframes · Encoding Hyperframes feedback that ends at an emoji boundary

Rudy Celekli’s engineering portfolio · AI-assisted summary

Problem

The feedback command could truncate between two UTF-16 surrogate units. URL encoding the partial emoji then threw an exception before the issue URL was available.

Engineering response

The truncator removes a trailing high surrogate if its matching low surrogate was cut off, while keeping the established limits and ellipsis.

Evidence and scope

A focused Vitest run found two baseline failures and recorded nine passes with the correction. Complete emoji at the boundary and ordinary ASCII were included as controls.
