the-value-engine-benchmark · Grading multi-turn enterprise negotiation

Rudy Celekli’s engineering portfolio · AI-assisted summary

Problem

Success in a multi-touch sales negotiation depends on decisions across the episode, which a single-turn score misses.

Design and implementation

The Value Engine Benchmark puts a model in the seller role against a stochastic buyer and applies a rubric after the negotiation episode. The repository publishes offline materials for inspecting evidence and methodology.

Evidence and scope

The source supports a benchmark of simulated negotiations. It supplies no live sales conversion measurement.
