Problem

A waitlist needs to transfer the information a visitor submits from the page into the intended intake system.

Design and implementation

Nabrix uses a Next.js landing page and a server-side API route documented as forwarding the form to AGNTCS CRM. The public repository exposes the intake architecture.

Evidence and scope

The source documents the handoff. CRM connectivity, deliveries, and conversion have no live measurement in this study.
