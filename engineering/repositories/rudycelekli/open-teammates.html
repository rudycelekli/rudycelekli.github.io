open-teammates · Durable work records for an event-planning agent

Rudy Celekli’s engineering portfolio · AI-assisted summary

Problem

A recurring events role needs continuity across planning, logistics and follow-up, including budget and attendee decisions.

Design and implementation

Open Teammates publishes Mira as an event-planning role with editable personality files, tooling, and a persistent work ledger. Artifacts make the planned workflow inspectable across steps.

Evidence and scope

The repository documents the role and artifacts. Effectiveness as an event operator remains an evaluation question.
