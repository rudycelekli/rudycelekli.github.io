Problem

A scorer exploit can matter differently once it becomes a training signal.

Design and implementation

Gradia Reward Loop carries oracle-witnessed pass/wrong pairs into a training-time experiment and records evidence bundles for replay. The project describes a paired GRPO diagnostic with single-variable localization.

Evidence and scope

The pinned source supports the documented diagnostic design. A broader effect on policy training needs independent replication and holdouts.
