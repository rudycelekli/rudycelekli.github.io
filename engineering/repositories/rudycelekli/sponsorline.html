Owned public project · rudycelekli/sponsorline

sponsorline · Auditing consent in a coding-tool sponsor channel

Rudy Celekli’s engineering portfolio · AI-assisted summary

Problem

Developers need a way to inspect whether an impression was displayed under consent and how it was selected.

Design and implementation

Sponsorline binds impressions to signed consent records and documents replay of auction decisions. The opt-in status-line design links the display to those records.

Evidence and scope

The repository describes an auditable workflow. Live payouts depend on identity verification and payment rails.
