gradia-range · Replayable adversarial tasks in synthetic twins

Rudy Celekli’s engineering portfolio · AI-assisted summary

Problem

Testing a risky agent workflow against a live system can expose operational data and make outcomes hard to reproduce.

Design and implementation

Gradia Range describes cloning a system into a synthetic twin, slicing it into witnessed tasks, and producing replayable verdicts. Humans and agents attempt tasks on separate boards.

Evidence and scope

The source documents the evaluation design and release timing. Synthetic coverage depends on how faithfully the chosen twin represents the workflow.
