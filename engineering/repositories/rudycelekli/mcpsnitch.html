mcpsnitch · Inspectable observations at the MCP boundary

Rudy Celekli’s engineering portfolio · AI-assisted summary

Problem

MCP tool descriptions alone give a reviewer limited evidence about the visible traffic produced by a tool session.

Design and implementation

MCPSnitch interposes between an MCP client and server, records visible protocol traffic, and can add best-effort process observations to a verifiable session report.

Evidence and scope

The public developer preview documents its observation model. Activity outside the instrumented boundary remains outside the report.
