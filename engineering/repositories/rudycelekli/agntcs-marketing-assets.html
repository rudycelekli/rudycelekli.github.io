agntcs-marketing-assets · A versioned public image handoff

Rudy Celekli’s engineering portfolio · AI-assisted summary

Problem

A marketing image can be difficult to identify when its only reference is an attachment or a mutable working link.

Design and implementation

The AGNTCS asset repository assigns committed paths to public Instagram imagery so a reviewer can refer to a specific revision when inspecting a creative handoff.

Evidence and scope

The repository describes an asset collection. The source does not establish campaign reach or asset performance.
