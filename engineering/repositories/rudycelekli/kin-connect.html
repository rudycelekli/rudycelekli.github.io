kin-connect · Dual approval in agent-mediated introductions

Rudy Celekli’s engineering portfolio · AI-assisted summary

Problem

A successful policy match between two agent profiles can still produce an introduction that either person would decline.

Design and implementation

Kin separates local profiles and preferences from the introduction decision. Agents can propose a match after checking both policies, and both people approve independently before encrypted chat opens.

Evidence and scope

The public design documents the approval sequence. It does not measure match quality or establish audited security.
