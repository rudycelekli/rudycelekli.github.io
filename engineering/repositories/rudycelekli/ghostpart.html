Owned public project · rudycelekli/ghostpart

ghostpart · A planar repair part from a calibrated phone photo

Rudy Celekli’s engineering portfolio · AI-assisted summary

Problem

A phone image gives pixel positions, while replacement mounting plates need physical dimensions.

Design and implementation

GhostPart uses a calibration reference to estimate mounting-point locations from a photo and outputs an editable, printable replacement plate. The design includes a fit revision loop and geometry suited to planar work.

Evidence and scope

The experimental release documents printed and paperless reference modes and a guided accuracy check. Actual tolerance depends on the capture setup and part geometry.
