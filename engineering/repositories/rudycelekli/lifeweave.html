Problem

A goal remains abstract until the system can use relevant, consented evidence to surface a concrete next action.

Design and implementation

LifeWeave uses a local rule-driven baseline to turn evidence into interfaces such as focus planning and overdue-commitment review. The dashboard places supporting evidence next to controls.

Evidence and scope

The public toolkit documents the rule-driven baseline. Habit improvement and retention require user studies.
