kya-evidence · Portable assurance for exact agent configurations

Rudy Celekli’s engineering portfolio · AI-assisted summary

Problem

A model or agent name leaves out the tool set, configuration, and policy needed to interpret an assurance check.

Design and implementation

KYA Evidence signs check receipts against an exact manifest and lets a relying party evaluate the receipts under its own policy. The reference implementation packages these inputs into dossiers for review and replay.

Evidence and scope

The public repository documents the reference format and evaluator controls. Industry adoption and independent assurance remain open questions.
