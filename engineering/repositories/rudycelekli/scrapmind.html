scrapmind · Planning inventions against a real inventory

Rudy Celekli’s engineering portfolio · AI-assisted summary

Problem

An invention plan can quietly require an absent part or allocate the same part to two physical roles.

Design and implementation

Scrapmind combines generated invention drafts with a resource assignment planner that records part roles, substitutions, and missing materials. A Device Alchemy flow adapts the planning idea to spare devices.

Evidence and scope

The source documents inventory-aware planning. Feasibility, safety, and actual fit still depend on materials and physical validation.
