gradia-universes-work-sample · Exactly-once replay after a simulated interruption

Rudy Celekli’s engineering portfolio · AI-assisted summary

Problem

An agent can resume its work using information that became stale while it was stopped.

Design and implementation

Gradia Universes models a synthetic world change and records the resulting visibility, interruption, restore, and authoritative-state checks. The fixture lets a reviewer inspect whether a change was applied exactly once.

Evidence and scope

The repository supplies a bounded synthetic work sample. The replay claim is scoped to that universe.
