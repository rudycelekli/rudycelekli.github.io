Gradia · Research artifact · Sep 2026

Reward Hacking in the RL Loop

In one paired GRPO/LoRA experiment on Qwen2.5-0.5B and GSM8K, the gameable reward passed 58 of 64 answers. The oracle confirmed one. The exact-match control held a zero proxy–oracle gap.

Scope · One seed and one model. Both arms lost oracle accuracy.

Gradia · Empirical preprint · Sep 2026

The Reward-Hacking Wind Tunnel

A stress test for benchmark graders found 89 false passes in 1,800 weak-grader attempts across 100 SWE-bench Verified tasks. Official tests rejected those patches.

Scope · Measures grader behavior against an oracle. Downstream training effects are an open question.

Gradia · Preliminary study · Aug 2026

Conditionally Approved

A synthetic underwriting environment changes facts, authority, and deadlines mid-task. The public study kept 37 gradable runs and tracked 18 infrastructure exclusions separately.

Scope · Four model judges showed low agreement (Fleiss’ κ = 0.151). Blinded human adjudication is pending.

Gradia · Study design and instrument · Aug 2026

The Value Engine Benchmark

An open benchmark for multi-touch enterprise negotiation with reproducible scoring. Its frozen evaluation grid covers 3,510 graded episodes across 13 models.

Scope · The preregistered judge–human agreement study remains open.
