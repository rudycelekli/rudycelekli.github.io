04

Claim policy

Strong claims need
strong boundaries.

I treat evidence quality as part of the engineering. A passing test is not deployment. A local demonstration is not external acceptance. Repository stars are not authorship. An agent’s report is not proof of completion.

Private work is represented only as a redacted aggregate unless disclosure is authorized. Public ownership and upstream contribution remain separate. Uncertainty stays visible.

Built
Source, release, and reproducible behavior are public.
Contributed
Upstream attribution or merge evidence is linked.
Measured
Method, scope, and uncertainty are stated.
Exploring
A question or prototype, not a completed claim.
